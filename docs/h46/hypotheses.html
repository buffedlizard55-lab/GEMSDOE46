physical signature local DFA exponent of the grid, measured along row and column transects in 12.8 km windows over scales 0.8-6.4 km, compared with its own background (31-window median) through a robust z-score; the transform is |z| (regime break) and |grad z| (regime boundary). Neither the field amplitude nor its gradient is used to form the ranking.

why off-catalogue a change in scaling regime is produced by a change in the *texture* of the source distribution - juxtaposed lithologies with different magnetic/gravity roughness, or hydrothermal alteration that suppresses magnetite along a fracture corridor - and it can be present where the amplitude contrast is too weak to make a sharp edge. The published catalogue was compiled from surface expression (scarp mapping); nothing in its compilation method targets a scale-invariance statistic.

differs from everything already in the repository no prior arm in any GEMSDOE repository uses a scale-invariance or DFA statistic. expected DTI proxy 0.0809 vs 0.0991 for the prior files at matched mass: measured to be WORSE on the only available off-catalogue proxy. Reported as a falsifiable negative result at the leaderboard operating point, and shipped as a separate artifact for the Final Prize Round and for expert review, not as the recommended scoring slot.

implementation cost low (CPU only, ~4 min for all 13 band-variant fields)

Maximize P(Win). Every choice is made against the official metric's own algebra (use the exact metric and state every emission-model assumption), never against a proxy known to drift.
