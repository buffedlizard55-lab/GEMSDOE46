1. H46-2 — DFA-corroborated multiphysics structural emission (SHIPPED, recommended for the scoring slot)

layers

  • official bands 1 mag_anom, 2 rtp, 3 tmi_hg, 6 tc, 12 det_elev, 13 iso_grav_anom, 14 tmi, 18 iso_grav_anom_hg (geometric-mean corroboration of 9 edge/curvature transforms)
  • 10% of the emitted mass reserved for the H46-1 DFA regime-break field
  • labels.tif only as the organiser-masked exclusion zone (200 m buffer)

physical signature agreement of independent edge/curvature transforms across two physics (potential fields and topography) - a corroboration filter, not a new transform

why off-catalogue the corroboration is asymmetric by construction: a location needs most transforms to agree, so a fault with a single strong signature (the catalogue's typical mode: a topographic scarp) is down-weighted relative to one that also perturbs the magnetic and gravity fields. Buried faults under basin fill have magnetic/gravity signatures with little topographic expression.

differs from everything already in the repository built here from the official bands only, with a geometric (not arithmetic) mean and an explicit DFA portfolio slice; no prior submission bytes are used. Correlation with the prior submissions is 0.31-0.36 and with tmi_hg/tmi_grad 0.75, so it is honestly reported as *not* a new hypothesis.

expected DTI proxy 0.1016 vs 0.0991 for the two best prior files at matched mass (37,654 px) and identical buffering; live expectation is anchored to the same operating point as the 0.2708-0.2778 family, i.e. ~0.27-0.29 with no evidence for >0.30

implementation cost low (CPU only, ~60 s)

validation VALIDATED on the off-catalogue proxy; hedge trade-off measured (0-20% hedge: 0.1054/0.1031/0.1016/0.1008/0.0990)

data no external data needed

2. H46-1 — DFA scaling-exponent break along magnetic and gravity transects (SHIPPED as the new hypothesis)

layers

  • official bands 1 mag_anom, 2 rtp, 14 tmi, 3 tmi_hg, 9 tmi_vg (magnetic group)
  • official bands 13 iso_grav_anom, 18 iso_grav_anom_hg, 5 iso_grav_anom_slope, 11 iso_grav_anom_vg (gravity group)
  • high-passed variants of tmi / rtp / mag_anom / iso_grav_anom at a 2 km scale

physical signature local DFA exponent of the grid, measured along row and column transects in 12.8 km windows over scales 0.8-6.4 km, compared with its own background (31-window median) through a robust z-score; the transform is |z| (regime break) and |grad z| (regime boundary). Neither the field amplitude nor its gradient is used to form the ranking.

why off-catalogue a change in scaling regime is produced by a change in the *texture* of the source distribution - juxtaposed lithologies with different magnetic/gravity roughness, or hydrothermal alteration that suppresses magnetite along a fracture corridor - and it can be present where the amplitude contrast is too weak to make a sharp edge. The published catalogue was compiled from surface expression (scarp mapping); nothing in its compilation method targets a scale-invariance statistic.

differs from everything already in the repository no prior arm in any GEMSDOE repository uses a scale-invariance or DFA statistic. Verified distinctness: smoothed Pearson -0.012 vs the 0.2600 file, -0.009 vs the 0.2778 file, -0.012 vs the h32d file, and -0.011/-0.007/-0.001/-0.011 vs the tmi/gravity/detrended-elevation gradient and Laplacian transforms; Jaccard overlap with the incumbent emission 0.0037 (276 shared pixels out of 37,654).

expected DTI proxy 0.0809 vs 0.0991 for the prior files at matched mass: measured to be WORSE on the only available off-catalogue proxy. Reported as a falsifiable negative result at the leaderboard operating point, and shipped as a separate artifact for the Final Prize Round and for expert review, not as the recommended scoring slot.

implementation cost low (CPU only, ~4 min for all 13 band-variant fields)

validation VALIDATED (as distinct) and NEGATIVE (as a score improvement) on the proxy instrument

data no external data needed

3. H46-3 — Hypocentre-lineament detector from the raw USGS earthquake catalogue (BLOCKED: needs one HTTP call from an unrestricted machine)

layers

  • external: USGS FDSN event web service, CSV export, region 37.5-41.5 N / 121.0-116.5 W, M >= 1.5, 1900-present
  • official bands 16 ieq_n100a15 and 10 deq_n100a15 used only as a cross-check (they are 100 km-radius smoothers and carry no 300 m-scale information)

physical signature spatial alignment of precisely located hypocentres into strike-parallel lineaments, projected to the surface, plus focal-mechanism nodal-plane consistency - a *fault-activity* signature rather than a rock-property signature

why off-catalogue the published label set is a Quaternary fault compilation; an active fault with no Quaternary surface trace (common where basin fill buries the range front, or where slip rates are low) can still generate microseismicity lineaments. The competition's own seismic bands are smoothed at 100 km and cannot express this.

differs from everything already in the repository the group's GEMSDOE32 wrote an equivalent fetch script but never obtained the catalogue; no shipped arm used raw hypocentres. The competition bands are used here only as a control.

expected DTI unknown - cannot be estimated without the catalogue; ranked third because the physical argument is strong and the data are free

implementation cost medium (lineament fitting plus holdout validation)

validation BLOCKED - not validated, so per the session rule it must not be given a submission slot yet

data VERIFIED OBTAINABLE IN PRINCIPLE: https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/query (free, official, no key). VERIFIED NOT OBTAINABLE HERE: the sandbox returns HTTP 000 for earthquake.usgs.gov (re-measured 2026-10-06). Run scripts/fetch_earthquake_catalog.sh on an unrestricted machine.

4. H46-4 — Windowed Euler deconvolution (structural index 1) of the isostatic residual gravity and RTP magnetics: depth-discontinuity alignment

layers

  • official band 13 iso_grav_anom, 2 rtp, 15 depth_to_base_surf, 18/5/11 gravity derivatives
  • no external data required

physical signature per-window Euler solutions (source depth and horizontal position) for a contact/step source (SI = 1); a fault produces a *lineament of consistent depth solutions*, which is a different statistic from an edge or curvature maximum

why off-catalogue Euler depth estimates respond to the depth extent of the source, so they flag basement-involved structures under cover, which the surface-mapped catalogue systematically under-records

differs from everything already in the repository prior arms applied Euler/tilt-depth ideas to magnetics as a scalar ridge (h32-1 'prethin-tip-euler', owner-reported 0.2649); this candidate requires *depth consistency along strike* across two independent potential fields, which no shipped arm tested

expected DTI moderate, unproven: the physics is standard but the group's related single-field variant did not beat its own incumbent

implementation cost medium (moving-window linear solve; CPU only)

validation NOT YET VALIDATED - implementation is feasible here, and it is the best next candidate that needs no new data

data no external data needed

5. H46-5 — Measured-geochemistry and hydrothermal-alteration signatures used as a REMOVAL (pruning) prior

layers

  • external: GDR wellspring chemistry (gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391, CC BY 4.0, DOI 10.15121/1881483), volcanic vents, Quaternary fault traces - all already restored in the group's mirrors
  • official bands 1 mag_anom (magnetic low from magnetite destruction) and 17 cond_surf

physical signature paired magnetic low plus elevated surface conductivity along a conduit, anchored on measured spring temperatures and geothermometer values - a *fluid-pathway* signature

why off-catalogue a conduit can be hydrologically active while its surface trace is absent or obscured; springs are direct evidence of a connected fracture network, and 15 of the 53 sites above 150 C measured by the group lie more than 3 km from any mapped trace

differs from everything already in the repository the group tested geothermometry as an *addition* arm (H33-1) and reported it falsified (credit per added dot 0.0022-0.0054 against a break-even bar of 0.054). This candidate uses the same data with the opposite sign - to *delete* mass - which is untested.

expected DTI small but positive if the removal rule is right: deleting k pure false-positive pixels at DTI 0.28 raises the score by roughly 0.2k/(0.2S + 0.8G) fractions of the current score

implementation cost low

validation NOT YET VALIDATED (pruning direction untested); the addition direction is falsified by the group's own measurement

data available in-repo mirror: data/external/gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv (restored); original free and official at gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391

How decisions are made here — the two core values.
Maximize P(Win). Every choice is made against the official metric's own algebra (DTI = T/(0.2·S + 0.8·|G|), marginal rule k > 0.2·DTI), never against a proxy known to drift. Where the proxy and the algebra disagree — they do, see IR-46-04 — the algebra decides and the disagreement is filed.
Own the Outcome. End to end: the shipped bytes are re-read and audited, the estimator is verified against exact-spectral fGn and textbook DFA values, negative results are published as negative results (H46-1 measured weaker than the structural field), and the instrument that would falsify the next idea is shipped with it.
