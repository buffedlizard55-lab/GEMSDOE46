Irregularities and unresolved issues

IR-46-01 [high] The 0.2778 attribution to h33-h33-2-b2 is contested

The session prompt attributes 0.2778 to gemsdoe32-h33-h33-2-b2. The live official leaderboard (retrieved 2026-10-06) does show 0.2778, but attached to participant 'extradr19' at rank 13, with no public file name or hash. The GEMSDOE32 site itself labels that artifact UNSCORED and gives 0.2747 as a model projection. The mapping file->score is therefore owner-reported, not an organiser receipt.

effect here The live-score calibration in registry/emission_model.json uses 0.2600 and 0.2778 as two members of one family. If the 0.2778 score does not belong to this family, the derived hidden-truth size (~14,087 px) shifts. The emission budget is therefore also anchored to the *measured* emitted mass of the best-known file (37,654 px) rather than to the fit alone.

flagged, not resolvable without a submission receipt

Page 967 says 'A sample submission that predicts total fault absence is provided'. The file restored here (sha256 2176d08e...) has 60,988 pixels equal to 1 and it is byte-identical to the catalogue raster (labels.tif), sha256 7ba308cc....

IR-46-04 [medium] Spatially blocked catalogue holdout is an inverted/worthless instrument for this competition

Measured here: at matched emitted mass, the file that scored 0.2600 live beats the file that scored 0.2778 live by 0.0659 vs 0.0330 on the held-out-catalogue blocks; the ordering is reversed versus the live board. The reason is structural: the organiser masks known faults, so emitting mass near them is neutral live but is *rewarded* by an instrument whose truth is the catalogue itself. The group independently measured Spearman +0.087 between this instrument and 11 live scores.

effect here Instrument A is reported but is never used for a decision. Decisions use the off-catalogue SGMC proxy (Spearman +0.31 on the same 11 files, group measurement) plus the live-anchored emitted-mass rule.

flagged, instrument discarded

IR-46-05 [high] The published DFA result does not transfer directly to a spatial grid

Varotsos et al. (2009) report a *time-lag* crossover in magnetic/electric field series (random at short lags, alpha ~ 0.9 at long lags). The session prompt frames this as the exponent shifting from ~0.5 to ~0.9-1.0 'right at the transition associated with the rupture process'. Applying DFA along raster transects to test for a *spatial* regime change is this project's adaptation, not the published result.

effect here H46-1 is presented as a new hypothesis with a literature-informed prior, not as a replication. Measured here, the median local exponent of the official potential-field bands is 1.00-1.95, i.e. the background regime of this dataset is not 0.5; the detector therefore tests *breaks relative to the local background*, never an absolute 0.5 threshold.

flagged, hypothesis scope limited accordingly

IR-46-06 [medium] This sandbox cannot reach most external data hosts

Only github.com and pypi.org were reachable. drivendata.org, dropbox.com, gdr.openei.org, docs.nlr.gov and earthquake.usgs.gov returned connection errors (openssl SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL / no route). The official pages quoted here were retrieved through the agent's web-fetch tool, not from the sandbox shell.

effect here No new external dataset could be downloaded and added in this session. The one external raster used (USGS SGMC traces) was restored from the group's public, hash-pinned mirror. Any hypothesis that needs new external data is listed as blocked with the specific source named (see hypotheses H46-3/H46-5).

flagged, environment limitation

IR-46-07 [low] The 'emitted size predicts the score' relationship is confounded

Across the group's 15 recorded (score, emitted-mass) pairs, emitted mass alone has Spearman -0.907 with the live score (their measurement, recomputed shape here). But those files are not independent: they are thinned/derived variants of a few related fields, and the metric algebra says size is only optimal at the point where the marginal pixel's realised credit falls below 0.2*DTI.

effect here Size is treated as an emission *decision under a model* (src/gems46/emission.py) with the live-validated operating point as an anchor, not as a causal law.

flagged

How decisions are made here — the two core values.
Maximize P(Win). Every choice is made against the official metric's own algebra (DTI = T/(0.2·S + 0.8·|G|), marginal rule k > 0.2·DTI), never against a proxy known to drift. Where the proxy and the algebra disagree — they do, see IR-46-04 — the algebra decides and the disagreement is filed.
Own the Outcome. End to end: the shipped bytes are re-read and audited, the estimator is verified against exact-spectral fGn and textbook DFA values, negative results are published as negative results (H46-1 measured weaker than the structural field), and the instrument that would falsify the next idea is shipped with it.
