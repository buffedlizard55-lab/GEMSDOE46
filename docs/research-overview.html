First-ranked experiment

Can deformation outline a buried fault?

H46-1 tests OPERA DISP-S1 line-of-sight displacement gradients: persistent, quality-screened steps may mark active structures or groundwater barriers beneath valley fill.

The official NASA catalogue returned OPERA products intersecting parts of the broad study rectangle, including a central-area 2025 granule. A separate Copernicus OData query returned S1A-only products and did not establish a repeat-pass pair. Scene files, full-grid coverage, and holdout behavior remain unverified.
