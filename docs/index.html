Why this is not another edge detector

Within 51.2 km row/column windows, DFA measures RMS detrended fluctuations at six scales from 0.8 to 12.8 km. R10 combines the difference between short- and long-scale slopes with departure from the local background exponent. It requires corroboration between raw RTP magnetics and isostatic gravity. No amplitude gradient or curvature enters its ranking.

The finite-scale anomaly might reflect buried structural texture, but also lithology, gridding or survey processing. It is not proof of a fault or geothermal vent. The broad window may explain poor 300 m localization.

What the 0.2778 result does—and does not—tell us

The owner's GEMSDOE32 attribution is not linked to a file by the official board. GEMSDOE32's own page still labels the file unscored. Sparse thinning can reduce false-positive cost without sacrificing much coverage, but the old README's inferred hidden-label counts and guaranteed causal explanation were not justified.

The exact identity is DTI = T / [0.2(T+S−M)+0.8G], not unconditionally T/(0.2S+0.8G). Better localization, coverage or precision could improve performance; this DFA experiment did not.
