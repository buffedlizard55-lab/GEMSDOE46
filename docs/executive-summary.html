Part 2: PhD-Level Autopsy of the 0.2778 Score & Path to 0.3195+

1. The Competition Metric: Distance-Tolerance Intersection (DTI)

The official evaluation metric is defined as:

DTI = TPw / (TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·FNw)

where $k(d) = \max(1 - d/300\text{ m}, 0)$ is a triangular tolerance kernel. In the sparse emission regime ($d \ge 300\text{ m}$), $FN_w = |G| - TP_w$ and $FP_w = S - TP_w$, which reduces to:

DTI = T / (0.2·S + 0.8·|G|)

The Marginal Credit Rule: Emitting an additional pixel increases the score if and only if its marginal credit exceeds $0.2 \cdot DTI$ (equal to $0.0520$ at $0.2600$, and $0.0556$ at $0.2778$).

2. Why GEMSDOE32's `h33-h33-2-b2` Scored 0.2778

DrivenData staff confirmed in official forum thread #11516 that known USGS/INGENIOUS faults are masked out of evaluation in both rounds. Consequently, emitting predictions on or immediately adjacent to mapped faults ($d \le 200\text{ m}$) earns $0$ credit ($k = 0$) while paying a false-positive penalty in the denominator ($0.2 \cdot S$).

In GEMSDOE32, deleting all dots within 2 pixels (200 m) of the known catalogue pruned 2,545 useless dots ($S: 40,199 \to 37,654$). This reduced the denominator by $0.2 \times 2,545 = 509$ without reducing credit $T$, causing DTI to jump from $0.2708$ to $0.2778$.

3. How to Reach 0.3195+ and 0.3345

Further mass thinning cannot bridge the gap to 0.3195+ because the remaining 37,654 dots are actively covering ground truth. Reaching 0.3195 requires $+15.0\%$ higher credit ($T: 5,223 \to 6,007$), and reaching 0.3345 requires $+20.4\%$ higher credit ($T \to 6,289$). This credit can only come from discovering unmapped blind faults using deep geophysical transforms like Detrended Fluctuation Analysis (DFA).

Part 3: The Detrended Fluctuation Analysis (DFA) Methodology

DFA (Peng et al. 1994, Phys. Rev. E) characterizes the fractal scaling exponent $\alpha$ of spatial series across scales $F(n) \propto n^\alpha$. In physical rock rupture (Varotsos et al. 2009, Chaos), shear fracturing causes a breakdown in scaling behavior, shifting $\alpha$ from uncorrelated ($\approx 0.5$) to strongly correlated ($\approx 0.9 - 1.0$).

We compute sliding-window DFA transects along rows and columns across 9 magnetic and gravity bands, evaluating local Z-scores against a 24.8 km background median and taking the regime-boundary transform $|\nabla z|$.
