IR-46-04[medium] Spatially blocked catalogue holdout is an inverted/worthless instrument for this competition

Measured here: at matched emitted mass, the file that scored 0.2600 live beats the file that scored 0.2778 live by 0.0659 vs 0.0330 on the held-out-catalogue blocks; the ordering is reversed versus the live board. The reason is structural: the organiser masks known faults, so emitting mass near them is neutral live but is *rewarded* by an instrument whose truth is the catalogue itself. The group independently measured Spearman +0.087 between this instrument and 11 live scores.

effect here Instrument A is reported but is never used for a decision. Decisions use the off-catalogue SGMC proxy (Spearman +0.31 on the same 11 files, group measurement) plus the live-anchored emitted-mass rule.

flagged, instrument discarded

IR-46-05[high] The published DFA result does not transfer directly to a spatial grid

Varotsos et al. (2009) report a *time-lag* crossover in magnetic/electric field series (random at short lags, alpha ~ 0.9 at long lags). The session prompt frames this as the exponent shifting from ~0.5 to ~0.9-1.0 'right at the transition associated with the rupture process'. Applying DFA along raster transects to test for a *spatial* regime change is this project's adaptation, not the published result.

effect here H46-1 is presented as a new hypothesis with a literature-informed prior, not as a replication. Measured here, the median local exponent of the official potential-field bands is 1.00-1.95, i.e. the background regime of this dataset is not 0.5; the detector therefore tests *breaks relative to the local background*, never an absolute 0.5 threshold.

flagged, hypothesis scope limited accordingly

IR-46-07[low] The 'emitted size predicts the score' relationship is confounded

Across the group's 15 recorded (score, emitted-mass) pairs, emitted mass alone has Spearman -0.907 with the live score (their measurement, recomputed shape here). But those files are not independent: they are thinned/derived variants of a few related fields, and the metric algebra says size is only optimal at the point where the marginal pixel's realised credit falls below 0.2*DTI.

effect here Size is treated as an emission *decision under a model* (src/gems46/emission.py) with the live-validated operating point as an anchor, not as a causal law.

IR-46-13[high] Official band 6 ('tc') is the GeoDAWN radiometric total count, not a magnetic curvature

The official GeoTIFF band description for band 6 reads 'tc - Tilt angle or total curvature - magnetic field derivative for edge detection'. Measured in this checkout on the hash-pinned rasters: band 6 correlates +0.997 with the GeoDAWN contractor 'TC' (total count) radiometric channel, +0.915 with Th, +0.881 with K and +0.715 with U (ScienceBase item 657e1d85d34e23d3533209f7, DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ), while its correlation with the magnetic bands is |r| <= 0.001. Band 6 is also undefined (-3.4028234663852886e38) exactly where the radiometric survey has no data, on a subset of the footprint where the magnetic bands are defined.

effect here Any transform that reads band 6 as a magnetic curvature or tilt angle is reading a radiometric count. The R11 radiometric family treats the K/Th/U grids on their own terms and uses band 6 only as a cross-check. Prior arms that consumed band 6 as 'tc = curvature' are not silently reinterpreted.

flagged; measured, not organizer-confirmed
