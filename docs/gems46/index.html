Core values

Maximize P(Win)

Every decision targets the probability of winning the prize, not a proxy. Where the metric's arithmetic offers a choice between a safe local number and a higher-variance shot at the top of the board, the shot is taken — but only after it is validated by an instrument that is out-of-sample.

Own the outcome

Every number on this site comes from a script in the repository and carries its own audit trail. Negative results are published, not buried: the full leaderboard inversion that failed is in Evidence.
